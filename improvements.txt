Objective: Build the "Naqsha Alif / Bay / Jeem" module, which allows users to import land and owner records (from the Punjab Zameen portal or spreadsheets), validate and normalize the data, save it hierarchically, and reuse it across other modules.
(Note: Let's complete Step 1: Data Ingestion, Mapping, Validation, and Storage first before moving to the final PDF generation).

1. Data Ingestion & Field Mapping

Allow users to input two distinct datasets (Land Details and Owner Details) using either:
• Direct Paste: Copying tabular data directly from the Punjab Zameen web portal into the app.
• File Upload: Importing spreadsheet files (.xlsx, .xls, .ods, .csv).
Auto-Mapping & Manual Override:
The system should automatically detect and map columns to the required fields below. If a column cannot be recognized automatically, display an interactive mapping UI so the user can manually assign the fields and ignore extra/unneeded columns.
• Dataset A: Land Details
	• Khewat No.
	• Khatoni No.
	• Square No. (Murabba)
	• Killa No.
	• Type of Land (Qism Arazi)
	• Area (Kanal-Marla-Sarsahi/Sq. Ft.)
• Dataset B: Owner Details (Ignore extraneous portal columns)
	• Owner Name & Relation/Parentage (Parse strings formatted like: [Owner Name] s/o, d/o, w/o, widow of [Father/Husband Name])
	• Owner Caste (Qaum)
	• Owner Share (Hissa)
	• Owner Area according to share (Raqba)

2. Automated Calculations & Data Validation (Per Khewat)

Group the imported records by Khewat No. and perform the following logic:
• Total Calculations:
	• Total Land Area = Sum of the area of all Square/Killa entries in that Khewat.
	• Total Owner Area = Sum of the area allocated to all owners in that Khewat.
• Discrepancy Check (Checkage):
	• Compare Total Land Area vs. Total Owner Area.
	• If equal: Display status as 1/1 (Valid).
	• If unequal: Flag an error clearly showing the mismatch ratio (Total Owner Area / Total Land Area) and highlight which total is incorrect.
• Share Normalization (Hissa Correction):
	• Because shares copied from the web portal are often corrupted, recalculate each owner's share mathematically using their area and the Khewat's total area.
	• Formula: Convert areas to the smallest standard unit (e.g., Marlas or Sarsahis). If an owner has 1 Kanal (20 Marlas) and the total Khewat is 4 Kanals (80 Marlas), output the corrected share as 20/80. Apply this to all owners.

3. Hierarchical Data Storage

Allow users to save the validated Khewat records under a structured location hierarchy:
• District \(\rightarrow \) Tehsil \(\rightarrow \) Qanoongoi \(\rightarrow \) Mauza \(\rightarrow \) Khewat No.

4. Cross-Module Integration ("Valuation & Taxes" Page)

Enable users to import saved records by Khewat No. directly into the "Calculate" and "DC / FBR Rate" tabs on the Valuation & Taxes page.
When importing into Valuation & Taxes, filter out owner details and extract only the land data based on the property classification:
• Rural Properties:
	• Required: Mauza, Square No., Killa No.
	• Optional: District, Tehsil, Qanoongoi.
• Urban Properties:
	• Required: Revenue Circle, Property Area, and either Khasra No. or Square No. + Killa No. (these two are mutually exclusive—if Khasra is provided, Square/Killa are not required, and vice versa).
